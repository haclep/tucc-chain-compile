# METHOD -- chain compilation, truncation ledger, and translation

This note records the conventions, the two small theorems the artifact
relies on, the two measured negative results that shaped the solver, and
the final solver design. Everything stated as "measured" is reproducible
from this repo; the numbers quoted are from `results/` as committed.

References used throughout:

- **P2**: Xu, Lee, Freericks, *Mod. Phys. Lett. B* (2020) -- factorized
  UCC on the Hubbard model; Eq. (6) is the Givens-block form of one
  factor; Table 1 gives closed-form coupled angles for L=4.
- **P1**: Freericks, *Symmetry* (2022) -- per-factor UCC/CC
  disentangling identity and the |theta| < pi/2 window.
- **ECS**: Evangelista, Chan, Scuseria -- exactness of ordered products
  of exponentials; ordering is part of the ansatz (their point T-7 is
  cited in-code as "T-7").

## 1. Conventions and signs

Spin-orbitals are indexed `2*m` (up) and `2*m+1` (down) for momentum
`m = 0..L-1`; determinants are bitmasks; the sector is fixed
`(N_up, N_dn)`. `Substitution(holes, parts)` is the primitive excitation
`A = prod a\dag_{parts} prod a_{holes}` with both tuples sorted and
disjoint; fermionic parity is computed by explicit occupation counting in
`dets.parity` (see CLAUDE.md for why this repo carries its own sign
code). All arithmetic is real: eigenvectors of the real symmetric H are
real, and each factor is the real rotation generated by
`kappa = A - Adag`.

The global phase is fixed so the pivot amplitude is positive; a final
`gsign` records any flip needed, so "fidelity" below always means the
positive overlap `<target|psi>`.

## 2. The primitive factor and the blocks lemma

For a fixed substitution, `A Adag + Adag A` is the projector `P` onto the
span of determinant pairs `(d_low, d_up)` with `A d_low = s d_up`,
`s = +-1`, and `kappa^2 = -P`. Hence

```
exp(theta kappa) = I + sin(theta) kappa + (cos(theta) - 1) P
```

which acts as the 2x2 rotation of P2 Eq. (6) on every block and as the
identity on fixed points. **Blocks lemma**: the blocks of one
substitution are disjoint, so the action is an exact sparse rotation
(`factors.apply_ucc_factor`; index arrays cached per substitution in
`SectorBasis.block_arrays`). Everything downstream -- the ledger, the 1-D
greedy closed form, the Gauss-Newton Jacobian -- is this lemma used three
ways.

## 3. Disentangling and the angle window (why |theta| < pi/2)

P1's per-factor identity,

```
exp(theta (A - Adag)) = exp(tan(theta) A)
                        * exp(-ln cos(theta) (A Adag - Adag A))
                        * exp(-tan(theta) Adag)
```

is verified to machine precision in `tests` (`apply_cc_triple` vs the
rotation). It diverges at `|theta| = pi/2` (the rotation annihilates the
reference's block component). The compiler therefore hard-bounds every
angle at `pi/2 - 0.02`, so the CC-side amplitude `t = tan(theta)` exists
and is at most ~50 in magnitude. Angles with `|theta| > pi/4` are flagged
`warn_gt_pi4` in the ledger (large but safe); `warn_gt_pi2` cannot occur
by construction in `sd_routed` and is checked for in `direct`.

## 4. Direct mode and the ledger theorem

`mode="direct"` eliminates target amplitudes into the pivot one Givens
rotation at a time, highest rank first (`rank_major`). Working at the
current top rank, the rotated pair `(pivot, mu)` has no side blocks at
that rank, so the update touches only the two listed amplitudes.

**Ledger theorem.** After K elimination steps, the state prepared by the
reversed K-prefix has fidelity with the target exactly equal to the
pivot amplitude recorded in the ledger at step K.

*Proof sketch.* Each elimination is an exact rotation in the
(pivot, mu) plane sending `(c_p, c_mu) -> (sqrt(c_p^2 + c_mu^2), 0)`;
composing the inverses of the remaining N-K rotations onto the target
reproduces the intermediate vector, whose overlap with the pivot is by
construction that recorded amplitude; reversing the K-prefix onto the
pivot gives the same number by orthogonality of the discarded
complement. The tests assert equality to 1e-9 at every K on a grid; the
measured deviation is 0.0.

Consequence: the direct truncation curve is **monotone in fidelity**
(each step strictly grows the pivot amplitude). The energy column need
not be monotone -- and measurably is not at small K for L6 U6, where
removing high-rank amplitudes first *raises* the variational energy
before it helps. Both facts are printed with the tables.

## 5. Why greedy SD-Givens elimination fails (a 2-cycle)

The obvious SD-only algorithm -- repeatedly eliminate the largest
amplitude reachable from the pivot by a single generalized S/D rotation,
routing higher ranks through intermediates -- **cycles**. Minimal
counterexample: P2's own L=4, U=8 lattice. Instrumented trace (in the
development transcripts, reproducible by re-enabling the naive loop):
eliminating a rank-2 amplitude `zeta` with pivot angle theta = -0.269
side-refills a rank-4 amplitude `mu4` to |c| = 0.0662 through the
rotation's side blocks; eliminating `mu4` through intermediate `nu2`
bleeds weight back into `zeta`; the pair repeats exactly. The Givens
move conserves weight inside every 2-block, and side blocks move weight
*both ways* -- a pivot-safety filter is insufficient. P2's Table 1 says
the same thing analytically: the closed-form L=4 angles satisfy coupled
relations (theta_4 = atan(gamma/alpha) - atan(tan^2 theta_3)); SD-only
angles must be solved **jointly**, not greedily one at a time.

## 6. The orbit obstruction (measured), and tangent completion

Joint solving over the routed tree is still not enough. On L=4 U=8 the
bounded Gauss-Newton stalls at residual 8.722e-3 from every start and
every jitter -- the same floor each time. That is not a landscape
artifact; it is an **invariant**:

- The ground state's K-sector has dimension 10 (all 10 determinants in
  the support).
- The 9 strictly-downhill routed tree edges generate a Lie closure whose
  orbit through the pivot has tangent dimension **8** -- a
  codimension-1 submanifold of the 9-sphere. The target lies off it;
  0.99564 is the maximum overlap on the orbit, and 1 - 0.99564^2 is
  exactly the observed floor.
- The **full** K-conserving generalized-SD pool (40 generators,
  lateral/same-rank moves included) gives orbit tangent dimension 9:
  universal.

The measurement script is small (build `kappa` matrices, orthonormalize
brackets to closure, rank the tangent maps at the pivot) and worth
keeping in mind whenever a solver "mysteriously" floors: check whether
the floor moves under restarts before blaming the optimizer.

**Cure -- tangent completion.** When the residual persists, append
general K-conserving S/D edges scored by `|<residual, kappa_e psi>|`:
the first-order fidelity gain of adding that letter at the end of the
word. This is a matching-pursuit step on the Lie algebra; on L=4 it adds
4 lateral doubles and the solve closes to machine precision.

## 7. The solver (mode="sd_routed")

Phase 0 -- **routing**. One S/D factor per support determinant, parent =
largest-amplitude determinant within 2 electrons at rank r-1 or r-2;
synthetic intermediates are inserted recursively when a rank gap cannot
be bridged; the sequence is restricted to the target's K sector when the
target is K-pure, and sorted rank-ascending (stable).

Phase A -- **greedy anytime ordering + initialization**. For one factor
applied to the current state,

```
<c_t| exp(theta kappa) |psi> = (A - C) + B sin(theta) + C cos(theta)
A = <c_t|psi>,  B = <c_t|kappa psi>,  C = <c_t|P psi>
```

maximized in closed form at `theta* = atan2(B, C)` (clamped to the
bound). Each step appends whichever remaining factor achieves the best
overlap. The prefix fidelity of this initialization is monotone by
construction, and it lands within residual 1e-2..5e-1 of the target --
an excellent, cheap start.

Phase B -- **joint bounded Gauss-Newton**. Solve all angles at once on
`F(theta) = prep(theta) - c_t` with the exact Jacobian
`dpsi/dtheta_k = U_N ... U_{k+1} kappa_k U_k ... U_1 |pivot>` (one
kappa insertion per column; blocks lemma again), Levenberg damping, and
the reparameterization `theta = (pi/2 - 0.02) * tanh(x)` which
hard-enforces the angle window. Expressiveness is bought with **letters,
never with larger angles**.

Phase C -- **growth**, when a floor remains: (i) bound-saturated letters
(`|theta| >= 0.92 * bound`, hence `dtheta/dx ~ 0`, a frozen column) are
split by appending a duplicate so two half-angles share the rotation;
(ii) tangent-scored edges as in section 6. Re-solve; repeat up to 10
rounds. Jittered restarts are the last resort and were not needed on any
committed system.

Measured outcome with canonical tie-breaks (all exact, residual
<= 2e-15): L4 U8 -- 13 doubles, max|theta| 0.787; L6 U2 -- 84 doubles,
0.611; L6 U6 GS -- 76 doubles, 1.549; L6 U6 root 2 (K=3) -- 84 doubles,
1.526. The two U6 chains each carry one flagged bound-saturated letter
(tan theta ~ 45-50, inside the window).

## 8. Anytime vs joint: a real tradeoff, not a bug

The prefix-fidelity curve of a jointly-solved SD word is **not
monotone**: measured minima along the committed chains dip to ~0.45-0.84
before closing at 1.0. Three attempts to have both properties at once
were implemented and rejected with data: greedy-anchored polish (drifts
along flat directions; dips return), frozen greedy head + jointly-solved
tail (tail stalls; full-word fallback erases the head property), and
progressive stage freezing (converges, but at 105-177 letters vs 76-94,
and still falls back on hard targets). Conclusion adopted by the
artifact: `sd_routed` optimizes for the shortest exact bounded-angle
word; if you need a graded, anytime-truncatable chain, use
`mode="direct"`, whose monotonicity is a theorem (section 4). Both
truncation tables are emitted side by side. Ordering is part of the
ansatz (ECS, T-7); re-sorting a solved word is not a legal fix.

## 9. Translation semantics (state-level, honestly capped)

`translate.cluster_analysis` performs exact cluster analysis of the
compiled chain state: `|psi> = exp(T) |pivot>` with T extracted rank by
rank by the standard recursion (rebuild error 0.0 on the committed
systems). The reported per-rank norms for L6 U6 GS --
`|T2|^2 = 0.966, |T3|^2 = 0.157, |T4|^2 = 0.172, |T6|^2 = 0.029` -- are
the honest inventory of what an SD cap discards. The SD-capped rebuild
`exp(T1+T2)|pivot>` scores fidelity 0.986730 and dE +197.494 mt against
exact; those two numbers ARE the translation distortion and are printed
next to every exact figure, never silently.

This is **state-level** translation. Operator-level normal-ordered
translation -- carrying P1's disentangling through the whole product and
re-normal-ordering into a single exp(T) operator identity -- is the
roadmap item; its per-factor seed (`apply_cc_triple`) and the
`t = tan(theta)` bridge are already in-tree, and the amps JSON stores
theta so downstream consumers can apply the bridge losslessly.

## 10. Diagnostics glossary

- `fid_after` (ledger): fidelity of the K-prefix preparation with the
  target; equals recomputed value to 0.0 by test.
- `residual`: `1 - <target|psi>^2` of the full chain, clamped at 0.
- `dE_mt`: variational energy error of the truncated state, in units of
  1e-3 t.
- `pct_corr`: percent of the correlation energy (pivot -> exact)
  recovered at truncation K; can be negative (see section 4).
- `warn_gt_pi4` / `warn_gt_pi2`: angle magnitude flags; the latter is
  structurally impossible in `sd_routed`.
- `grown` / `restarts` (solver_info): letters added by growth; jitter
  restarts consumed (0 on all committed systems).
- `rank_counts`: factor count per substitution rank; `sd_routed` is all
  rank <= 2 by test.

## 11. Stage 1: operator-kernel certification and the dressing law

`disentangle.py` wires the chain artifacts into the operator-level
translation program of P1. Three certification levels, run by
`examples/run_stage1.py` after the main driver (results in
`results/stage1_certification.md`):

**C1 -- independent kernel.** A tuple-determinant kernel ported from the
upgradation symbolic engine (determinants as sorted index tuples, parity
counted on tuples) with the composite operator ordering pinned to
`dets.Substitution` (annihilate holes ascending, create parts ascending;
adjoint exactly reversed). Kernel and bitmask/vector machinery agree on
every committed chain to <= 2.2e-16 -- two independent implementations
of the fermionic sign algebra certify each other end to end.

**C2 -- interchange fidelity.** The committed `chain_*.json` files
round-trip to their eigenstates with infidelity 0.0; the schema is a
faithful carrier of the compiled artifact.

**C3 -- the operator-valued dressing law, measured to its boundary.**
On two-factor probes the law is EXACT in this pipeline (tests): the
earlier letter's amplitude is tan(theta_a) * sec(theta_b) whenever the
later letter shares ANY spin-orbital -- occupied or virtual -- and the
later letter stays tan(theta_b), matching P1 eqs. 38/46 and the
upgradation symbolic engine's two-factor measurements. The naive product
extension to deep chains FAILS, and the failure is quantified: median
relative error on pivot-anchored amplitudes (later-secants / all-secants
/ bare tan theta) is 0.40 / 0.76 / 0.45 for the 13-letter L4 chain,
2.15 / 2.25 / 0.16 for L6 U2 (84 letters), and 5.5e3 / 3.1e4 / 1.6 for
L6 U6 (76 letters, whose bound-saturated letter contributes sec(theta)
~ 46 and detonates every product containing it). On a jointly-solved
chain nearly every letter shares orbitals with most later letters, so
the secant product over ~50 sharing letters over-counts what the
dropped folded terms would compensate;
bare tan(theta) sits closer because the jointly-solved angles already
absorb the interaction numerically. Composite ranks are entirely folded:
chain-letter coverage of ||T_r||^2 is 1.000 at r = 2 and exactly 0.000
for T3, T4, T6 on every system -- P1's warning that a low-rank
factorized-UCC ansatz "will not typically map to a low-rank CC
approximation", now with numbers.

Consequence for the ov-CC roadmap (upgradation Q1): an order-1
truncation of the operator-valued amplitudes cannot be a per-pair secant
product over deep chains -- the folded excitation products are the
leading structure, not a correction. Candidate composition laws should
be scored against this harness; it is the regression bed Stage 1 was
missing.

## 12. Prefix spin contamination (measured, both modes)

Every primitive generator conserves N_up, N_dn (hence S_z) and -- under
K-pure routing -- total momentum at EVERY prefix, but nothing in either
compile mode constrains S^2 away from the endpoint: the compiler
enforces the abelian symmetry structurally and leaves the non-abelian
one free. The solver uses that freedom. Measured on the L6 U6 singlet
ground state (`diagnostics.prefix_s2_curve`; the pre-canonicalization
chain's curve, peak 1.240, was reproduced digit-for-digit across
Windows/OpenBLAS and Linux -- the canonical chain below regenerates
identically everywhere by construction):

- `sd_routed` (76 letters, canonical): <S^2> peaks at **1.837 at
  K = 30**; grid values (K, fid, S^2) = (10, 0.477, +1.552),
  (20, 0.480, +1.778), (30, 0.449, +1.837), (40, 0.807, +0.496),
  (50, 0.942, +0.375), (60, 0.834, +0.078), (70, 0.999, +0.001),
  (76, 1.000, 0.000). On the order of half the norm and more sits
  outside the singlet manifold mid-chain, and the contamination tracks
  the fidelity dips -- two views of the same fact that jointly-solved
  intermediates are not physical states. The peak is chain-dependent in
  detail (1.240 on the pre-canonical chain) but O(1) robustly.
- `direct` (220 letters): peak **0.292 at K = 83**, settling below
  ~0.05 over the last hundred letters. Milder and decaying, but the
  monotone-fidelity truncations are still mildly spin-mixed objects.

Doctrine amendment: truncation curves in BOTH modes are fidelity and
resource diagnostics, not spin-pure physical approximations. Endpoint
uses (exact compilation, translation, amps export) are unaffected; the
endpoint singlet purity |<S^2>| < 1e-6 is a regression test for both
modes (`test_endpoint_singlet_purity`).

Design tension for any Stage-2 fix: spin-ADAPTED generators are sums of
primitive substitutions, which breaks the single-A structure
(kappa^3 = -kappa, disjoint 2x2 blocks) underlying both the closed-form
rotation and the per-factor tan(theta) CC bridge. The bridge-compatible
candidate is to keep primitive letters but apply symmetry-partner pairs
adjacently with tied angles (the exact state has symmetry-equal
amplitudes, so the constraint is natural and growth can absorb lost
freedom). That experiment has not been run; this section records the
measurement that motivates it.

## 13. Platform determinism: canonical tie-breaks

Symmetry makes exact ties ubiquitous: amplitudes related by spin flip
and momentum reflection are equal to machine precision, so any
`argmax`/`sort` over them resolves by BLAS rounding noise -- different
LAPACK builds legitimately pick different members (measured first on
the root-2 pivot quadruplet: chains of 87 vs 96 letters from the same
eigenstate on Linux vs Windows, both exact).

The cure is one rule applied everywhere a discrete choice is scored by
an amplitude: scores tied within a relative `TIE_TOL = 1e-9` are broken
by the SMALLEST deterministic key (a determinant mask, or a
substitution's (holes, parts)). Implemented as `_canon_argmax` /
`_canon_desc` in `compile.py` at every decision site: pivot selection,
routing support order and parent choice, the greedy factor pick,
growth's residual-top set and edge ordering, and direct-mode's
elimination pick. Direct mode additionally ignores support below the
fidelity-derived floor sqrt(fid_tol)/dim, whose elimination could only
add noise-sensitive null steps. `diagnostics.resolve_degenerate_roots`
extends the same discipline to the roots table: within each degenerate
eigenvalue cluster (followed past `nroots` so truncation never exposes
a mixture) the basis is rotated to the simultaneous (K, S^2) eigenbasis,
ordered by (K, S^2), and sign-fixed by the canonical dominant
determinant -- so multiplet rows carry pure, platform-stable labels per
ADR-003.

Regression tests (`test_determinism.py`): chains invariant under a
global eigenvector sign flip and under 1e-13 amplitude noise (two
orders above BLAS differences, seven below the tie tolerance) in both
modes, and pure-K labels for the L6 U6 degenerate pair.

Guarantee scope, refined by cross-platform testing: canonical
tie-breaks make every DISCRETE selection platform-stable -- the pivot,
the routing tree (order included), direct-mode chains in full, root
labels, and the exact multiset of sd letters. One channel is
irreducibly continuous: near a SYMMETRIC Gauss-Newton stall (the L4
orbit obstruction), the closest-point set on the orbit is a
symmetry-related pair of basins, and sub-tolerance input differences
select between them -- measured when a Windows/OpenBLAS run swapped the
two symmetry-partner L4 growth letters under 1e-13 noise while the
Linux build did not. Consequently the ORDER and angles of the sd
growth segment are canonical only up to symmetry-equivalent basins;
the noise regression asserts exactly the guaranteed layer (pivot,
letter multiset, length, exactness). Sign flips remain bit-exact
(canonical phase fixing makes compile(v) and compile(-v) identical),
and direct mode -- fully discrete plus closed-form angles -- keeps
strict structural equality. A symmetry projection of the stalled
residual before edge scoring would remove the basin choice entirely;
recorded here as future hardening. In practice all committed rendered
outputs have matched across Windows and Linux; the basin caveat is why
that match is an empirical observation for the sd growth segment
rather than a theorem.

## 14. sd_paired: the promoted spin-graded mode

`mode="sd_paired"` is the productionized Stage-2 configuration: routing
is overlap-ablated (section 13's repair pass as policy), non-self-
conjugate letters are applied as adjacent (A, F(A)) pairs with tied
angles (theta, sigma_A theta), growth is overlap-averse and F-closed,
and the joint solve is a tied bounded Gauss-Newton. Every letter remains
a PRIMITIVE rank<=2 substitution, so the per-factor tan(theta) CC
bridge (section 3) survives -- the property full spin adaptation would
destroy.

Measured properties (two independent full runs, Linux and
Windows/OpenBLAS; the L6 U6 singlet ground state):

- Exactness: converges to the repo contract with NO orbit obstruction
  (mid-push rank(J) saturates the full K-sector dimension); final
  residuals 3.6e-15 and 8.9e-13.
- Length: 261-427 letters vs 76 untied -- a 3.4-5.6x cost,
  trajectory-dependent (growth is part of the continuous channel).
- Spin grading: the contamination that remains is the ALLOWED (-1)^S
  channel -- quintet fraction 81.5-92.1% at the peak, forbidden
  (triplet) weight suppressed 5.1-12.6x vs the untied chain; endpoint
  <S^2> tracks the residual to machine precision (7e-15 measured).
- F-sector confinement: with ablated routing, min <F> along all
  prefixes = -1.0000 (4 letters on L6 are forced through the
  overlapping class; 0 on L4).
- Septet is structurally absent in the K=0 sector at L=6 (the lone S=3
  multiplet carries K=3); the projector machinery returns exact zeros
  there -- a free consistency check.

Cost note: L4-scale compiles are instant and CI-tested
(tests/test_paired.py); L6-scale compiles take minutes in pure Python
and were validated through the deadline-chunked battery implementation
of the identical algorithm (examples/exp_stage2_battery.py).

## 15. Constructive normal-ordered composer (translation stage i)

`normalorder.py` builds the operator-level translation constructively.
The finiteness insight that makes it tractable: no BCH series is ever
needed, because each factor is EXACTLY the degree-2 polynomial
exp(theta kappa) = I + s kappa + (c - 1)(A Adag + Adag A). A word is
therefore a finite product of small operator polynomials, normal-
ordered by exact Wick algebra: integer signs, contractions from
{a_p, a_p^dag} = 1, coefficients as exact integer polynomials in
{s_k = sin theta_k, c_k = cos theta_k}. Conventions are pinned to
dets.Substitution (math-order strings, quasiparticle classes relative
to an explicit reference occupation).

Verified (tests/test_normalorder.py): V1 -- the composed operator
matrix equals the independent tuple-kernel product to 1e-12 on every
tested shape, including generalized (lateral) letters; V2 -- the pinned
closed forms hold as EXACT polynomial identities, derived rather than
measured. Three laws now constructive:

- Secant dressing is the ABSENCE of a cosine: disjoint partners
  contribute s1 c2 to the earlier amplitude; sharing any orbital
  deletes the c2, and s1/(c1 c2) = tan(t1) sec(t2).
- Routed composites: for a cascade (0->2)(2->4), c0 = c1 only (the
  ref-inactive factor contributes no cosine) and the composite
  amplitude is s1 s2, i.e. t(0->4) = tan(t1) sin(t2).
- Disjoint doubles fold to a quad with coefficient exactly s1 s2
  (connected T4 = 0), the operator-level form of the disconnected
  product.

Complexity seeds (term counts per multiplication): a single is 6
monomials, a double 18; a disjoint-doubles product is 324 = 18^2 (no
collapse -- tensor structure), while sharing collapses hard (shared-occ
pair: 6). Stage (ii): translate the committed L4 13-letter chain and
match every determinant amplitude against the chain state at machine
precision, recording the term-count curve vs word length -- the number
that decides between an explicit folded expansion and operator-valued
amplitude functions as the production representation (stage iii).

## 16. Stage (ii): the committed chains, constructively translated

Both committed chains pass the constructive translation at machine
precision (tests/test_normalorder.py; results/noc_stage_ii.md): the L4
13-letter chain symbolically (max deviation 6e-17 over all amplitudes,
quad closed form = 9 exact pathway terms), and the L6 76-letter chain
via `compose_numeric` -- the identical Wick algebra with coefficients
evaluated at the chain's angles -- in ~4 s at 3e-16 over all 68
amplitudes.

Two structural facts, measured: (1) annihilator pruning is EXACT for
the reference projection (a monomial with annihilators can never become
creator-only under further left-multiplication), collapsing the
creator-projected composition to at most the reachable support;
(2) operator-monomial count saturates at that support even for the
FULL operator (L4: 898 of the 65536 bound, flat from step 11) -- the
collapsing regime, not tensor growth. The genuine growth axis is
symbolic pathway counting inside the coefficient polynomials (88-term
amplitudes at L4; prohibitive mid-word at L6 in 152 variables).

Stage-(iii) verdict, decided by these numbers: symbolic closed forms
are the right representation where pathway counts stay small
(few-factor identities and L4-scale chains); the numeric-coefficient
constructive evaluation is the production path for real chains. Both
are the same exact algebra over different coefficient fields.

## 17. H4: the molecular validation

`molecular.py` closes the original "H4/Hubbard" deliverable with a
self-contained, pip-only molecular layer: STO-3G hydrogen is s-Gaussians
only, so overlap, kinetic, nuclear attraction, and the ERI all have
closed forms via the Boys function F0; a plain closed-shell RHF, the MO
transform, and a sector-Hamiltonian builder that uses the stack's own
elementary-operator parity kernel. Two-level certification: externally,
H2 at 1.4 bohr reproduces the textbook STO-3G values E_RHF = -1.116714
and E_FCI = -1.137276 Ha to every printed digit; internally, the
identity <HF|H|HF> + E_nuc = E_RHF holds to ~1e-12 at every geometry,
certifying the integrals -> SCF -> transform -> H chain at once
(tests/test_h4.py). Psi4 remains the production integral source per the
roadmap; this layer exists so the validation is self-contained.

Results (results/h4_validation.md), rectangle 2.0 x 2.5 bohr and the
near-square 2.0 x 2.05 multireference stress point (|c_HF| 0.94 -> 0.76,
||T2||^2 0.13 -> 0.71): all three compile modes exact off-lattice
(sd_routed 15 doubles, direct 16 with H4's genuine 6 quadruple factors,
sd_paired 18), every constructive translation at ~1e-16 with
support-saturated term curves, endpoint singlets, bounded angles. First
exercise of the k_pure = None routing path (molecules carry no momentum
label); roots labeled (E, S^2, dominant det) per ADR-003 without K.
Nothing in the compiler, the paired mode, or the composer needed a
single change to leave the lattice.

## 18. Hydrogen-systems scaling: richer bases, bigger molecules

`molecular.py` now carries an `H_BASES` table and a general
`hydrogen_integrals(atom_centers, basis)` for multi-shell s-only bases
(`h4_integrals` remains as a back-compatible wrapper). 6-31G hydrogen
(split valence, two s shells) is the first basis upgrade; the p-function
boundary -- cc-pVDZ and beyond, or any non-hydrogen atom -- is exactly
the Psi4 integration hand-off, which makes the integration tier the
capability boundary rather than a convenience.

A bug story worth keeping: the first 6-31G run returned E_RHF = -5.5 Ha
for H2 while the certification identity <HF|H|HF> + Enuc = E_RHF stayed
clean at 1e-13. Internal consistency with absurd energies localizes the
fault to the integrals layer by construction -- and indeed the
generalized code was counting each nucleus once per SHELL in the
nuclear-attraction loop. One-line fix; the identity did precisely the
job it was designed for.

Validated systems (results/hn_scaling.md; tests/test_hn.py):
H2/6-31G reproduces the literature E_RHF = -1.126743 / E_FCI =
-1.151679 Ha. H4/6-31G rectangle: sector dim 784, support 208,
|c_HF| = 0.9485, E_FCI = -2.125153, identity 8.5e-13, direct compile

## 19. Profiling the molecular sd frontier -- and moving it

Instrumentation on a truncated H6 state attributed 100% of wall time to

1. Column-batched Jacobian (`apply_ucc_factor_cols`): the per-letter

   with an identical trajectory.

## 21. Frozen core / active spaces (rung one toward heavier dimers)

## 22. Rung three, part one: Davidson + the compiled H-application

`fastpath.py` adds the two ingredients that lift the dense-
diagonalization bound. The Davidson method converges only the lowest
few roots by repeatedly applying H to a small set of trial vectors --
the full matrix is never built. Its one requirement, a fast
"H times vector" routine, walks the second-quantized terms over the
determinant bitmasks directly and is compiled to machine code by numba
when installed (the `[fast]` extra); without numba the identical
algorithm runs in pure Python, slower. Both paths are held to the
dense builder by equivalence gates (tests/test_fastpath.py): matvec
and diagonal at < 1e-12 on committed systems, Davidson eigenvalues at
1.9e-14 against dense diagonalization on H4/6-31G (dim 784, 43
matvecs for three roots) and the H6 ring (dim 400, 42 matvecs).

A bug story for the record, in the same genre as every previous one:
the first Davidson re-orthogonalized the whole search space with a QR
factorization after each expansion, which may flip signs of EXISTING
columns while their stored H-images keep the old signs -- corrupting
the vector/image pairing. Dimension 784 passed by sign luck; the H6
ring's degeneracies exposed it (energies wrong by 0.64). The fix is
the standard discipline: newcomers are orthogonalized against the
existing set (twice), which is never touched -- pairing cannot
desynchronize by construction. The repair also collapsed the matvec
count 310 -> 43.

Measured cost law: one warm compiled matvec at dim 784 with ~16,500
terms is ~78 ms; cost scales as dim x terms. Forecast: H10 (5,5),
dim 63,504, lands at minutes per matvec -- so part two's required
optimization is precomputed sparse connectivity (walk the terms once,
store the nonzero couplings, then each matvec is a sub-second sparse
multiply), alongside the resumable chunked sd driver that the C2
chain needs. Cr2 (12e, 12o) at 853,776 determinants sits one further
factor beyond, reachable by the same construction with symmetry-block
restriction.

## 22c. Rung three, part two: the resumable chain compiler

`examples/run_big_sd.py` turns one long chain compilation into many
short invocations: rerun the same command until it prints DONE. Every
phase -- sparse eigensolve (with degenerate-mixture projection on the
sparse adjacency), routing, greedy ordering, joint angle solve, growth
rounds, jittered restarts, and the constructive translation --
checkpoints its progress to a pickle beside the dump at EVERY phase
transition and at fine grain inside the long phases (greedy every 50
letters, the solver every slice, the translator every 40 letters).
Termination signals are converted to catchable interrupts so the final
save always writes. The solver never commits to a work unit it cannot
afford: a two-iteration PROBE slice measures the per-iteration cost on
the actual problem, after which every slice is admitted only if 1.3x
its measured cost fits the remaining budget.

The loop is a faithful mirror of `_compile_sd` -- same routing, same
canonical greedy, same growth scoring and restart seed -- and the
guarantee is a test, not a claim: with deadlines disabled
(`mirror_exact`), the driver must reproduce `compile_chain` on the
reference system exactly (same pivot, same word, angles to 1e-10;
tests/test_fastpath.py). With deadlines active, slicing perturbs only
the continuous channel (damping restarts per slice); exactness of the
final chain is unchanged.

## 22d. The wrong-root incident: the seeding law and leak-triggered purity

The first overnight C2 run compiled the wrong eigenstate, exactly, and
reported success. E0 landed at -74.63998153 -- +49.77 mHa above the
known ground -- with 1216 creator monomials, the 2 x 608 signature.
The dense root table identified it: an arbitrary mixture of the exactly
degenerate first excited triplet pair (S^2 = 2, the two Pi spatial
components in two H-disconnected blocks of 608), the (8e,8o) analog of
C2's famous low-lying a3Pi_u. Every downstream gate passed because the
machinery was exact for the state it was given; only the target was
wrong.

The mechanism is a theorem, now a law: a Davidson search is
variationally sealed inside the H-disconnected blocks its starting
vectors touch -- H is exactly zero across blocks, so the residual, the
diagonal preconditioner, and the orthogonalization all preserve block
membership, and a converged residual is fully compatible with total
blindness to a lower state in an untouched block. The shipped driver
seeded from the lowest DIAGONALS (nroots=2, default seeds), and
equilibrium C2 is the first committed system where the sigma/pi
competition puts the lowest diagonals outside the ground state's block:
four open-shell pi determinants, exactly tied by symmetry, two per Pi
block. The seeds went to the Pi blocks; the solver honestly converged
the lowest thing it could see.

The projection safety net failed for an independent reason: its
trigger was a ROOT-GAP test (w1 - w0 < 1e-9). By elimination -- no
projection fired AND the compiled support was 1216 -- the equilibrium
dump couples the two Pi blocks above the 1e-10 walk threshold
(pi_x/pi_y symmetry breaking at RHF-convergence noise scale). In that
regime either the noise-split gap exceeds 1e-9 and the branch is
skipped while the TRUE numerical eigenvector genuinely spans both
blocks (avoided crossing at zero detuning forces the 50/50 mixture),
or the gap stays under 1e-9 but the 1e-10 walk crosses the noise
couplings, fuses the blocks, and finds no leak to project.

Fix, in examples/run_big_sd.py: (1) the HF determinant ALWAYS joins
the three lowest-diagonal seeds, covering both regimes at once --
equilibrium-type grounds (HF block) and stretched-type grounds
(lowest-diagonal blocks) -- with nroots = the seed count; (2) purity
of the selected root is decided by LEAKED WEIGHT, not root gap: walk
the block from the root's dominant determinant at threshold 1e-7
(above integral noise, below any coupling that matters at our
tolerances), project if the out-of-component norm exceeds 1e-8, and
pay the price in public -- the post-projection eigen-residual is
computed and reported; (3) the report now persists the target
diagnostics that made this a three-session diagnosis instead of one
glance: all tracked roots to 8 decimals, support, dominant-block size
at both walk thresholds, routed count, projection status, HF-block
membership. Regression anchor: tests/test_seed_fix_mini.py builds a
three-block miniature shaped exactly like the trap and, against the
real davidson(), reproduces the failure, verifies the fix, and
exercises both projection regimes (exact degeneracy and the 1e-8
noise bridge).

Verification on the real system: the reseeded init printed roots
-74.68975162 / -74.63998153 / -74.63998153 / -74.58131327 -- ground
recovered to every dense digit, the old wrong answer sitting behind it
as the degenerate pair, digit for digit. The pair's split is below the
printed 8 decimals; with the leak trigger covering both noise
mechanisms, which one operated is archival, not operational. The
fourth root also corrected a misprediction worth recording: the
tracked roots are the lowest states OF THE SEEDED SECTORS, not the
global lowest k -- the third triplet (-74.638974 in the dense table)
lives in a block no seed touched.

The wrong night's chain is preserved, not deleted:
data/c2_2348_triplet_pair_chain.npz (2767 letters, ranks {2: 2726,

## Genealogy and certificates

### Lineage

Three papers and one gap define where this method comes from.

Xu-Lee-Freericks (Mod. Phys. Lett. B 2020) established the
backwards construction: take a known exact eigenstate (the
four-site Hubbard ground state) and build, by hand, an ordered
product of individual UCC factors that prepares it exactly --
including the rank-4 factor of its Table 1, which direct mode
still reproduces on the P2 lattice. Everything the compile stage
does (route, angle-solve, order, hit the eigenvector exactly) is
the mechanization of that construction.

Freericks (Symmetry 2022) supplies the exact factor algebra: a
single-substitution UCC exponential terminates in closed form,

    U(theta) = I + sin(theta) kappa + (cos(theta) - 1)(A Adag + Adag A),
    kappa = Adag - A,  via  kappa^3 = -kappa,

so every chain letter is EXACTLY a degree-2 operator polynomial --
no BCH series anywhere in the stack. The same paper's operator-
valued first-order amplitude relations (tan dressed by secants
over sharing letters; eqs. 38/46/51) are honored here as a scored
hypothesis, not an engine: stage-1 certification C3 measures where
the first-order law holds and where folded (multi-factor) terms
take over.

Evangelista-Chan-Scuseria (JCP 2019) is the adjacent formal prior
art: a disentangled (factorized) UCC product can represent any
state exactly, and ordering matters. Existence and formalism there;
explicit construction for physical eigenstates in XLF; algorithmic
construction plus exact translation here.

The tucc contribution closes the gap between XLF and Symmetry:
the constructive normal-ordered composer multiplies the full chain
out by exact Wick algebra over the closed-form factors, retaining
every folded term, yielding the exact wave operator Omega at
arbitrary chain length. The variational cousin (tune angles against
the energy with no eigenvector; Chen-Cheng-Freericks) is the
planned tucc.chains.grow branch, deliberately NOT this pipeline:
here the eigenvector is the input by design, because exactness
certificates require a known target.

### Omega vs T

The stored translation object is the wave operator Omega =
c0 I + sum_mu c_mu E_mu with Omega|HF> the exact state; c0 equals
the reference amplitude (sqrt of the dominant weight -- the
identity-monomial check in every floor probe). Cluster amplitudes
in the e^T sense follow by finite algebra, T = log(Omega/c0)
(cluster_analysis), terminating exactly in a finite space. Exact
amplitudes satisfy CC's projected equations identically; nothing
is ever solved downstream of the eigensolve.

### The certificate chain

Doctrine: no number that matters has only one path into existence.

### Residual currencies

The two drivers report "residual" in different units; never
tabulate them in one column unconverted.

  dense (compile_chain / run_psi4_dump):
      final_residual = 1 - fid^2   (fidelity deficit)
  sparse (run_big_sd):
      residual = ||chain|HF> - v0||   (vector norm)

For fid -> 1: norm ~ sqrt(deficit), since ||psi - ct||^2 =
2(1 - fid) and 1 - fid^2 ~ 2(1 - fid). E.g. a dense 8.9e-16
deficit corresponds to a ~3e-8 norm. Dense sd exactness is
certified by C3 acceptance against the eigh vector; direct-mode
entries are certified at the deficit level only.
